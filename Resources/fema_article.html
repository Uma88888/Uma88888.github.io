How to Check Flood Risk Before Buying Property

Published: June 2026

Why Flood Risk Matters

Flood risk can affect insurance costs, construction options, property value, and future development.

How to Check Flood Risk

One of the easiest ways to begin is by reviewing FEMA flood maps. These maps show areas with different levels of flood risk and are freely available to the public.

Public Data Sources

  • FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer
  • County GIS Maps
  • USGS Topographic Maps

The example below shows a parcel located partially within a mapped floodplain.

Things to Remember

  • Flood maps are only one part of a property evaluation.
  • Local drainage conditions may differ.
  • Additional investigations may be appropriate before purchase.
