How to Use USDA Soil Maps

Published: June 2026

Why Soil Matters

Soil conditions can influence drainage, septic system suitability, foundation performance, vegetation, and future land use. Reviewing publicly available soil information is often a useful first step when evaluating a property.

What Information Is Available?

The USDA Soil Survey provides detailed information about mapped soil types and many of their characteristics, including drainage, hydrologic soil groups, depth to restrictive layers, flooding frequency, and engineering interpretations.

Public Data Sources

  • USDA Web Soil Survey
  • USDA SSURGO Soil Database
  • County GIS Mapping Portals

Things to Remember

  • Mapped soil units are generalized and may vary across a property.
  • Public soil maps do not replace site-specific investigations.
  • Different projects require different soil characteristics.
  • Soils should be considered together with terrain, flood risk, and other site conditions.
